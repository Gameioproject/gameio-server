Delete your account

This permanently deletes your Gameio account, its devices and sign-in tokens, synced saves, play history, favorites, collections, likes and blocks. Your comments are blanked and shown as deleted. This can't be undone.

Games downloaded to your devices aren't touched. You can also delete your account in the app under Settings → Gameio → Delete account.
