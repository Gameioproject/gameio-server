Privacy policy

Last updated 17 September 2026

This policy explains what the Gameio Android app and the playgameio.com service collect, why, and how you can delete it. Gameio has no ads, no analytics trackers, and never sells your data.

What we store on our server

  • Account: your username, email address if you give one, your password (stored only as a secure hash), an optional avatar, your app preferences, and when you last signed in or used the service.
  • Devices: the device name, app version and sync settings of each device you sign in on, the IP address it connected from, when it was last seen, and the sign-in tokens that keep it connected.
  • Your games: favorites, collections, play history (which games you played, when and for how long), and the save files and save states you choose to sync.
  • Community: comments you post, likes, reports you file, and users you block.
  • Connected services: your RetroAchievements username and progress, if you connect it.
  • Logs: our servers and our network provider keep short-lived technical logs (IP address, time, requested address, app or browser version) to keep the service secure and working.

What stays on your device

  • Downloaded games, your local game folders, emulator settings and app settings.
  • Add-on files you import. When you open a game, the app asks each add-on's own web address for download sources, so that add-on's host sees your IP address.
  • A Real-Debrid account key, if you add one. It is stored encrypted on the device and only ever sent to Real-Debrid.

Why we use it

To run your account, show the catalog, sync saves and play history between your devices, show and moderate comments, keep the service secure, and fix problems. We don't use your data for advertising or profiling.

Services we rely on

  • Hetzner (Germany) hosts our server, and Cloudflare delivers and protects our websites.
  • Game covers and screenshots load from IGDB, and trailers from YouTube, which see your IP address when they load.
  • When you download a game, the app connects to the host named by your add-on (for example the Internet Archive or Real-Debrid). Emulator cores and updates download from their publishers, such as libretro and GitHub. RetroAchievements is contacted only if you connect it.

These services handle data under their own privacy policies.

How long we keep it

We keep your account data until you delete your account. When you do, we delete your account, devices, sign-in tokens, saves, play history, collections, likes and blocks straight away. Your comments are blanked and show as deleted, so replies from other people still make sense. Copies in our database backups are replaced within 30 days, and technical logs are kept only for a short time.

Deleting your account

In the app, open Settings → Gameio → Delete account, or use playgameio.com/delete-account. You'll need your username and password.

Security

All connections use HTTPS, passwords are stored as hashes, and account keys on your device are encrypted. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it by writing to dev@playgameio.com. Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

Gameio is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
