For Android 8.0 and up.
A Gameio account is required.

  1. 01

    Install Gameio.

    Download the APK. If Android asks, allow installation from your browser.

  2. 02

    Sign in. Settle in.

    Use your Gameio account, choose your systems, and select where your games are stored.

  3. 03

    Bring your games.

    Import one or more add-on files during setup or in Settings. You can also copy games into your platform folders, then explore the catalog and find your next favorite.
